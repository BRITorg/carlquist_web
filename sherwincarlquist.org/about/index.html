About This Site

This website is a preserved archive of the original www.SherwinCarlquist.com, the personal site of botanist Sherwin Carlquist, showcasing his research on plant discoveries, island biology, wood anatomy, and floral morphology.

The original site was mirrored on July 2, 2020. That mirror is the basis for what you see here, which has since been republished so the content remains publicly accessible. Further details about the web archive are avaiable at https://github.com/BRITorg/carlquist_web/.

The content, images, and links are preserved as they were originally captured; some assets or external links from the original site may no longer resolve, and the publication PDFs referenced throughout the site are not hosted here. A small number of technical repairs have been made since 2020 to keep the site working correctly — for example, restoring broken images and a broken slideshow. Each publication's [ PDF ] link now expands in place to show where the paper can be found instead — a DOI, a journal or repository link, and/or a Wikidata record — matched against the Carlquist Publications Dataset. None of Dr. Carlquist's original text has been altered.

About The Sherwin Carlquist Extended Specimen Network

This site and web archive are preserved and shared as part of a collaborative project to link and preserve Carlquist's specimens, field images, publications, and archival materials. Details about this project and further Carlquist resources are available at https://britorg.github.io/carlquist_esn/.
